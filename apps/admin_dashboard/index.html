1. Anti-Leakage & Operational Decoupling

At forecast initialization time $T_0$, future reference observations do not exist. Many academic models accidentally leak future observations into feature pipelines. The implemented leakage gate and temporal validation tests are designed to prevent future reference/error information from entering $T_0$ inference.

2. Lead-Dependent Dynamic Bust Thresholds

Project labeling thresholds scale dynamically with lead horizon $\tau$:
Threshold(τ) = Base_Threshold · [1 + 0.12 · max(0, (τ - 24)/24)] Base thresholds: Precipitation 25 mm, Temperature 4.0°C, Wind 8.5 m/s. Compound bust logic triggers when absolute error exceeds the lead-scaled threshold.
