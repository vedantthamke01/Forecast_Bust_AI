## The Pitch Script

> "Medium-range weather forecasts can fail catastrophically—costing lives and resources even when the initial forecast looks plausible. 
> 
> For problem SIH26079, we built an AI-based forecast reliability layer that works alongside Numerical Weather Prediction models. 
> 
> At forecast issuance time, our calibrated LightGBM model evaluates synoptic consistency, ensemble spread, and seasonal dynamics to calculate the exact probability that a medium-range forecast will bust. 
> 
> The judge sees a live operational dashboard with calibrated bust risk, TreeSHAP explainability, and spatial risk maps across 25 Indian cities—giving forecasters actionable confidence before issuing critical advisories."
